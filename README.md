## Shamba market
## Note ...shamba market is just a class demo 

An online marketplace for a farmers' cooperative. Farmers advertise their agricultural products, and buyers can browse them, add items to a cart and place an order. Payment and delivery are **simulated**: the system records the order and shows a confirmation, but no real money moves.

## Project background

A farmers' cooperative wants a simple way to connect its members with potential buyers. This system covers the core buying journey, from logging in to receiving an order confirmation.
